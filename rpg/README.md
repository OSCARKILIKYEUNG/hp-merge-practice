# Hogwarts — Charms in Motion

A landscape 16:9 Charms action RPG trial in the Hogwarts Courtyard and Cloister.

Move with WASD, dodge with Space and use R for Ultimate. Tap a Wand, merge matching Elements, choose a Spell, prepare it with the Book, then drag the ready Charm from its board cell to an enemy or your Health. On phones, use the left movement stick and the right-side board. English and Japanese are available.

This folder contains the production website and runtime models. Editable TypeScript, Blender sources, original asset provenance and launch instructions are provided separately in the project source archive. The earlier Merge games remain at the parent website with independent progress.

This is one playable Vertical Slice, not the complete school or novel campaign. Physical phone performance and native background recovery still require device testing.
