# Hogwarts — Magic in Motion

A landscape 16:9 action game in the Hogwarts Courtyard and Cloister.

Move with WASD or Arrow Keys, roll with Space, cast Fire with J or a left click, cast Wind with K, heal with H and use R for a charged Ultimate. On phones, use the left movement stick and Roll, then the right-side spell buttons. Point or tap in the world to aim; spell buttons can turn toward a nearby enemy. Spells fly straight from the right-hand Wand. English and Japanese are available.

Magic regenerates automatically and each skill displays its cooldown. Dodge enemy attack warnings, defeat the Courtyard guards, then follow the objective to the Warden. Zero Health means defeat; Try again restarts the trial. Progress uses hp-action-rpg-v1 and preserves the previous Charms and Merge saves.

This folder contains the production website and runtime models. Editable TypeScript, Blender sources, original asset provenance and launch instructions are provided separately in the project source archive. The earlier Merge games remain at the parent website with independent progress.

This is one playable Vertical Slice, not the complete school or novel campaign. Physical phone performance and native background recovery still require device testing.
